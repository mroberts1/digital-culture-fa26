---
title: "Chopped"
---

**Chopped** is a slang term used to describe something or somebody unattractive, ugly, or nasty. Its derivative **choppelganger** describes an uglier, off-brand, or lesser version of something.

### Origin

The term "chopped" was first put onto Urban Dictionary in 2014 by R. Lavish, though it was not well-known as internet slang at that point. It was mainly popular in AAVE (African American Vernacular English), but others describe it as being popular in the New Jersey and New York areas, mainly to describe something as messy or unkept. On Urban Dictionary, the term was originally defined as "A girl or boy that's ugly". The term skyrocketed back into the cultural zeitgeist in mid-late 2024 through TikTok.

### Usage

The word is quickly being implemented into everyday conversation and has evolved past the social media landscape. "Chopped" is now used to describe something or somebody unattractive, ugly, or nasty. In a more recent Urban Dictionary definition, chopped is defined as, "an ugly ass biatch", but the word can also be used situationally and can also be a personal feeling. It can be used to describe someone else, as well as yourself. Oftentimes, when referring to oneself, people will say something along the lines of, "I look so chopped right now", or "this outfit makes me feel chopped". When referring to others, it is often used as an insult, substituting the word "ugly" or "gross".

The "#chopped" hashtag on TikTok has gotten a lot of traction, with many videos getting nearly, or even over, a million views. When referring to oneself, people also use chopped as a way to [[Oversharing|fish for compliments]], specifically on social media apps such as Instagram or, more likely, TikTok.

### "Optionally chopped"

One TikTok trend that made the term "chopped" more mainstream was the online trend where people posted an ugly version, either a picture or video, of themselves and contrasted it with a more attractive or more "done-up" version of themselves. This trend was given the title of "optionally chopped". One of these videos, done by famous influencer Haley Baylee, has over three million views, where she shows a video of herself with no makeup and big glasses, contrasted with a video of herself at a red carpet event, wearing an evening gown and full-glam makeup. This trend has garnered millions of views on individual videos and has reached the larger influencer world, pushing the term further into mainstream lingo.

### Choppelganger

The word "chopped" evolved, and its push into mainstream culture has led it to be implemented into other words. A "choppelganger" is a new term coined by Gen Alpha which is a portmanteau of the contemporary digital phrase "chopped" with the classic phenomenon of a "doppelganger". The term chopped means ugly or unattractive, and the word doppelganger means a look alike of something. Therefore, the combination of the two words describes an uglier, off-brand, or lesser version of something. Overall, the word encompasses the concept of the "less attractive twin", a common theory in today's cultural zeitgeist.

The term is often used in reference to dupe or counterfeit items and celebrity lookalikes. For example, if someone buys a bad and obvious dupe of a luxury brand bag, the off-brand bag could be called the more attractive and on-brand bag's choppelganger. A YouTube video which features a man on a talk show decoding [[Brain rot|Gen Alpha slang]] references the term choppelganger in a segment called "what are the kids saying?", where they define a choppelganger as a "less attractive version of something". The term was also decoded by *People* magazine, and even implemented in a game in *The New York Times* entitled "Test Your Internet Brain. Can You Use 'Choppelganger' in a Sentence?" In fact, people are now craving to discover their choppelgangers, so much so that a website entitled choppleganger.com serves the main purpose of helping people find their "ugly" celebrity lookalikes.
