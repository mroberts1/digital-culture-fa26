**Looksmaxxing** is an internet slang term used to describe the practice of maximizing one's physical attractiveness through various forms of appearance related improvement. The term is rooted in its association with online incel communities and the broader manosphere, where physical attractiveness is often treated as a determining factor in romantic and social success. Looksmaxxing generally involves attempting to improve one's perceived attractiveness through grooming, exercise, skincare and cosmetic procedures. Despite its original association with these niche online communities, the term itself has expanded into mainstream internet culture, where it is frequently used both seriously and ironically to describe the pursuit of physical self improvement. This terminology has also become a way of mocking the obsessive appearance based standards and perceived [[Mogging|masculine hierarchies]] associated with incel culture.

### History and origins

The term looksmaxxing is derived from the expression "maximizing," which refers to the process of improving or optimizing a particular bodily characteristic. Its terminology is also closely associated with the gaming expression minmaxing, which describes maximizing desirable attributes while minimizing weaknesses. Looksmaxxing originated within online male self improvement communities during the early 2010s, particularly through forums associated with incel culture and the broader manosphere. One of the earliest documented uses of the term is associated with Lookism.net, an online forum established in 2015 which focused on male attractiveness and physical appearance. The terminology subsequently spread across 4chan and other online communities concerned with facial aesthetics, dating and perceived genetic advantages. According to Know Your Meme, the term appeared in a dedicated Lookism.net discussion in June 2015 before becoming increasingly common across other online platforms.

Within these communities, looksmaxxing developed alongside the concept of the blackpill, an ideology which emphasizes the perceived importance of physical attractiveness and genetic characteristics in determining romantic and social success. Individuals participating in these communities frequently discussed facial measurements, height and other physical characteristics as indicators of attractiveness. Looksmaxxing was consequently presented as a method of improving one's position within an appearance based dating hierarchy.

The term eventually expanded beyond its original association with incel communities. During the early 2020s, looksmaxxing became increasingly visible on TikTok and other social media platforms, where creators began producing appearance rating videos and physical transformation content. Its mainstream popularity also introduced the terminology to audiences who were unfamiliar with its original subcultural associations.

### Types of looksmaxxing

Looksmaxxing is generally divided into two categories, softmaxxing and hardmaxxing. These distinctions describe the different methods through which individuals attempt to improve their physical appearance.

**Softmaxxing** refers to relatively conventional methods of improving physical attractiveness without undergoing invasive procedures. These practices generally involve skincare, exercise, hairstyling and dental hygiene. Individuals may also change their clothing or dietary habits in an attempt to improve their overall appearance. Although these practices are associated with looksmaxxing communities, they largely overlap with conventional grooming and personal fitness.

**Hardmaxxing** refers to more invasive attempts to alter physical characteristics, often through cosmetic surgery or other medical procedures. Rhinoplasty, chin augmentation and hair transplantation, as well as bone smashing, are examples of procedures discussed within looksmaxxing communities. More extreme discussions include unverified techniques such as bonesmashing, which involves repeatedly striking facial bones under the false belief that doing so will improve facial structure. Such practices can cause serious physical injury and are not supported by reliable medical evidence.

### Facial aesthetics

A significant portion of looksmaxxing culture revolves around evaluating facial characteristics according to perceived standards of attractiveness. These discussions frequently borrow terminology from anatomy, orthodontics and cosmetic surgery, although their interpretations are not always scientifically accurate. Commonly discussed characteristics include canthal tilt, facial symmetry and midface proportions. Canthal tilt describes the angle of the eyes in relation to their inner and outer corners, while facial symmetry refers to the degree of similarity between the two sides of the face. The jawline receives considerable attention, particularly through discussions of the mandible and maxilla of the lower and upper jawbones. Some communities also use the term PSL rating, an informal numerical system used to rank facial attractiveness. These ratings attempt to establish a standardized hierarchy of physical attractiveness, although they lack scientific validity and are based largely on subjective assessments.

### Mainstream internet culture

The expansion of looksmaxxing into mainstream internet culture has significantly changed how the term is widely used on the internet. While it originally described a serious practice within incel and male self-improvement communities, it has increasingly become incorporated into internet memes and humorous discussions of physical appearance in a way that is mocking of incel communities. The suffix "maxxing" has also developed into a broader form of internet slang, allowing users to describe the act of maximizing almost any activity or characteristic. Expressions such as "gymmaxxing," "sleepmaxxing" and "moneymaxxing" extend the original terminology beyond physical attractiveness. These variations are frequently used without any direct association with incel ideology.

The terminology has also become a way of mocking the communities from which it originated. Social media users frequently imitate looksmaxxing vocabulary to parody obsessive discussions of facial attractiveness, masculinity and dating. Referring to an ordinary activity as looksmaxxing or describing someone as a professional "looksmaxxer" can function as an ironic reference to individuals who treat everyday social interactions as competitions in physical attractiveness.

The distinction between sincere participation and ironic usage has consequently become increasingly important. Someone using the terminology may genuinely subscribe to looksmaxxing ideology, participate in the culture for entertainment or simply repeat popular internet slang without understanding its origins. This expansion demonstrates how terminology associated with fringe online communities can acquire entirely different meanings as it circulates through mainstream social media.

### Criticism

Looksmaxxing has attracted criticism because of its emphasis on physical appearance as a measure of personal value. Although many of its practices involve conventional self improvement, more extreme interpretations can encourage obsessive appearance monitoring and unrealistic beauty standards. Its association with blackpill ideology has also raised concerns about the reinforcement of misogynistic beliefs and genetic determinism.

Another concern involves the potential relationship between appearance focused communities and body dysmorphia. Constantly evaluating facial characteristics against rigid standards can encourage individuals to perceive ordinary physical features as defects requiring correction. The commercialization of looksmaxxing has further contributed to these concerns, as influencers and cosmetic providers begin to profit from these insecurities.
