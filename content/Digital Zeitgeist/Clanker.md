---
title: Clanker
---

**Clanker** is an internet slang term and pejorative primarily used to describe robots, artificial intelligence (AI), or automated systems. Originally popularized in the context of science fiction and gaming, the term has recently seen a massive resurgence in contemporary digital culture as a derogatory label for AI-generated content, automated social media bots, or individuals perceived as lacking human empathy.

### Etymology and origins

The term derives from the onomatopoeic word "clank," referring to the sound of heavy metal striking metal. While the word has been used historically to describe loud mechanical noises, "clanker" was heavily popularized as a fictional slur by the *Star Wars* franchise. In the animated series *Star Wars: The Clone Wars*, clone troopers frequently used the term to refer to battle droids (such as the B1-series battle droid). In this context, it was used by organic, human soldiers to assert dominance and express contempt for their mechanical counterparts.

### Cultural impact in gaming and media

In the 2010s and 2020s, the term was widely adopted by gaming communities. In first-person shooter games featuring mechs or robots, such as *Titanfall* and *Apex Legends*, players commonly use "clanker" to mock robotic enemies or players who utilize highly mechanical or automated playstyles. It serves as an in-group marker, allowing human players to draw a distinct line between human skill and artificial or mechanical behavior within the game's universe.

### Contemporary internet usage and AI anxiety

With the rapid rise of generative AI tools like ChatGPT and Midjourney in the mid-2020s, the use of "clanker" experienced an exponential surge on platforms like Reddit, TikTok, and X (formerly Twitter).

In this modern digital context, "clanker" is frequently used to express technophobia or general discontent with the proliferation of AI. Internet users deploy the term to criticize AI-generated art, automated customer service bots, and algorithmically driven moderation systems. For example, users might comment "Get this clanker out of my feed" under a video suspected to be generated by AI.

Furthermore, the term has evolved beyond machines. It is occasionally used as an insult toward humans who behave in a cold, unfeeling, or highly mechanical manner, implying that they lack basic human warmth or are acting like corporate robots.

### Sociological implications

The modern usage of "clanker" reflects a broader societal tension regarding the automation of labor and the increasing role of AI in daily life. As human-AI interaction becomes more ubiquitous, "clanker" functions as a linguistic defense mechanism. By dehumanizing AI through derogatory slang, users are attempting to draw a sharp boundary between human authenticity and artificial simulation. It highlights a growing cultural anxiety about the attention economy and the loss of human-centric spaces on the internet.
